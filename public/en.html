🎮 Game Room Rules

  1. Bookings must be made via the hotel's online QR system.
  2. Service time is limited to 1 hour per room.
  3. Keep volume at a moderate level to avoid disturbing other guests.
  4. Choose age-appropriate games for players.
  5. Do not modify settings or unplug any equipment. Report issues to staff immediately.
  6. Food and drinks are strictly prohibited in the game room.
  7. Guests are liable for any damages caused by negligence and must compensate at full value.

🧊 Ice Bath Rules

  1. There is a 50 THB fee per session. Please pay and receive instructions at the counter before use.
  2. Users must be in good health. Individuals with heart conditions, high blood pressure, asthma, or epilepsy are prohibited.
  3. Pregnant women, individuals with open wounds, or those under the influence of alcohol/drugs are strictly prohibited.
  4. Session time in the room is 1 hour, but for safety, do not immerse in ice water for more than 5-10 continuous minutes.
  5. Exit immediately and notify staff if you experience dizziness, shortness of breath, severe shivering, or chest pain.
  6. Never plunge alone. Always have a companion or staff member nearby.
  7. Enter and exit the bath slowly and carefully to prevent slips and falls.
  8. Users assume all risks. The hotel is not liable for any health issues or injuries resulting from rule violations.
